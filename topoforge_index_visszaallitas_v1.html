THE PROBLEM

Interconnect can become the design.

In repeated processing-element networks, connectivity can consume a large share of LUT, routing and carry resources. TopoForge FPGA focuses on the structure of that network rather than treating every connection as equally necessary.

The current Technology Preview demonstrates a protected sparse fabric workflow on a validated reference configuration. It is deliberately narrow: the goal is to prove the delivery model before generalizing the optimizer.

WHERE TOPOFORGE FPGA MAY HELP

Good candidates are structured, repeated and connection-heavy.

TopoForge FPGA is aimed at designs where many similar processing elements exchange mostly local, structured or sparse information. These five areas are useful starting points for an evaluation.

01

Multi-sensor systems

Sensor arrays and condition-monitoring systems with many similar channels and mostly local data exchange.

02

Image & thermal-image processing

Local pixel or region processing where neighboring processing elements dominate the useful communication pattern.

03

Industrial multi-channel measurement

Parallel measurement and instrumentation architectures whose channels can be grouped into local processing neighborhoods.

04

Robotics & distributed sensing

Distributed sensing and control fabrics with repeated nodes, local interactions and selective long-range communication.

05

Event-driven & sparse-activity systems

Fault detection, alarms and event-driven processing where only part of the network is active or relevant at a given time.

These are candidate application classes, not guaranteed savings. The benefit depends on the actual connectivity, data path, timing constraints and FPGA implementation.
